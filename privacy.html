Morning Brief – Privacy Policy

Morning Brief is a personal tool used only by its owner, Thierry Muller, to send himself a daily summary on Telegram.

Data accessed: read-only access to the owner's Google Tasks (task titles and due dates).

Use: tasks due today or overdue are included in the owner's private daily message. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Data is not stored beyond the time needed to send the message.

Revoking access: at any time via Google Account → Third-party access.

Contact: via the owner's GitHub profile, github.com/thimith.
